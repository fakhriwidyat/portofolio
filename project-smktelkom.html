SMK Telkom Redesign (JHIC)

WEB DEVELOPMENT • COMPETITION

Project Overview

A modern redesign of the SMK Telkom Purwokerto website, developed specifically for the JHIC competition. The project focuses on a cleaner layout, structured information architecture, and highlighting the school's identity as a digital tech institution.

My Role & Challenges

Working as the UI/UX Designer under a strict competition deadline, I was responsible for the research, wireframing, and visual design system that guided the development team. The biggest challenge was organizing a massive amount of school data—like admissions, majors, and announcements—into a simple, easily navigable menu structure, while keeping the visual identity consistent across every page.

Technologies Used

Figma for wireframing and prototyping, with the final design implemented in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by the development team.
